Model scope
North is -Z. Facilities of the Korea Institute of Nuclear Safety to the west/north
(including its tennis courts) are not part of the school model.
Roof colors, footprint dimensions, bridge locations and heights are approximate;
the small differences between each building's storey heights are simplified.
The game uses DJSHS to distinguish Daejeon from Daegu; this is a game identifier.
Satellite images and photographs are not distributed with the game.
2026-10-29 exterior/site refinement
- The Wikimedia Tami front-view photo listed below was opened and inspected.
  It shows ochre/tan brick, pale floor bands, a dark central accent, a partial
  white roof canopy and the neighboring red-brick dorm with white strips.
  Building-specific brick/panel palettes, window bands and the partial canopy
  replace the previous shared beige exterior. The dark clock/sign panel is not
  reproduced as a false interior or invented readable text.

  This September 2023 photo shows paired granite piers, horizontal darker bands,
  a retractable barrier and green perimeter fencing. The model uses stone-colored
  piers at the existing west entrance, plus a separate school sign and fencing;
  the barrier and guard booth are not asserted to have exact measured shapes.
- The central field remains muted grass with a worn center. Field perimeter
  walking routes and west vehicle access are distinct. All facade colors, pier
  heights, canopy dimensions, wall heights and path widths are visual estimates.
  Photographs are evidence only and are not distributed.
