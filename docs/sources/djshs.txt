Model scope
North is -Z. Facilities of the Korea Institute of Nuclear Safety to the west/north
(including its tennis courts) are not part of the school model.
Roof colors, footprint dimensions, bridge locations and heights are approximate;
the small differences between each building's storey heights are simplified.
The game uses DJSHS to distinguish Daejeon from Daegu; this is a game identifier.
Satellite images and photographs are not distributed with the game.
