Model scope
North is -Z. The main building is oriented about 52 degrees from the east axis.
The historical diagram predates Seohyeon. Its small rear block placement and
4-storey mass are tentative, not confirmed by a current labelled site plan.
Dormitory room boundaries, minor annexes and facade details are simplified.
Main/dormitory base-height difference uses the documented floor connection;
other grades, lengths and roof forms are visual approximations, not survey data.
No map pixels, illustrated diagram or source prose are bundled with the game.
2026-10-29 exterior/site refinement
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hansung_Science_High_School.jpg
  The 2023 photograph was viewed directly. It shows light cream/gray rendered
  science facades and a conspicuous broad green dome over a rounded front mass.
  The former tiny centered dome and three-storey rectangular front are replaced
  with a larger green dome over a shallow stepped approximation of the rounded
  frontage. The front is represented at the science block's five-slab height;
  its exact curved profile, structural floor levels and dome diameter remain
  approximate. Dome base now meets the roof rather than floating above it.
- Naver satellite view was revisited north-up, confirming the long hillside main
  block, the newer rear mass and a small green sport court southeast of the gym.
  A basketball court is added there. The current rear mass is visible, but its
  identification as Seohyeon and exact boundaries remain tentative.
- Retaining faces and field-edge railings emphasize the tall central terrace;
  road and walking routes follow the existing satellite trace. Gate piers are a
  conservative approximation at the known approach, not a measured gate design.
  Wall/fence heights, court dimensions, frontage subdivisions and all color
  samples are visually estimated. Sources do not provide a surveyed contour plan.
  Photos and map pixels are not distributed.
